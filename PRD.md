# 1. Product Overview

## 1.1 Product Name

**PRIMA WISNU ABROR AZMI — Personal Portfolio**

---

## 1.2 Product Purpose

Create a professional personal portfolio website that presents **PRIMA WISNU ABROR AZMI** as a technology and business-oriented professional who combines:

- Technology
- Business understanding
- Artificial Intelligence
- AI Prompting
- Critical thinking
- Problem solving
- Digital business development

The website must not primarily function as a traditional developer portfolio.

The website must not focus on:

- Project galleries
- GitHub repositories
- Programming languages
- Framework lists
- Technical certifications
- Large collections of screenshots

Instead, the website should communicate:

> **Who PRIMA WISNU ABROR AZMI is, how he thinks, what he understands, and what business he is building.**

---

# 2. Core Positioning

The primary positioning is:

> **Technology + Business + AI + Problem Solving**

The visitor should understand that PRIMA WISNU ABROR AZMI is capable of looking beyond the technical implementation of a solution.

The portfolio should communicate the ability to:

1. Understand a problem.
2. Understand its business context.
3. Analyze the underlying issue.
4. Determine whether technology is appropriate.
5. Use AI where it provides value.
6. Evaluate AI-generated results critically.
7. Refine solutions when necessary.
8. Turn technology capabilities into practical business value.

# 5. User Problems

The website should solve several visitor problems.

## Problem 1

A visitor may see technical skills but not understand the person's actual business value.

### Solution

Focus on capability and problem solving instead of technical skill lists.

---

## Problem 2

AI capability is often presented superficially.

### Solution

Explain AI Prompting as a structured thinking and evaluation process.

---

## Problem 3

A company may want to know whether the person understands business, not only technology.

### Solution

Highlight PT Primadev Digital Technology and business thinking.

---

## Problem 4

Traditional portfolios often contain too many projects.

### Solution

Do not make project showcases the core of the website.
